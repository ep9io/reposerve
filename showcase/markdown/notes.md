# Notes

A short second page. It gives the previous and next links at the foot of a page
a target. It also gives the tree a sibling.

## Why it is short

A page with three headings is enough to show the table of contents. The
contents panel lists every `h2` and `h3` on the page.

### A subsection

The panel indents a third-level heading, so the shape of the page is visible
without reading it.
